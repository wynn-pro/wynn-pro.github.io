SLICEMASTER

The dojo throws things in the air. You slice them in half. How hard can it be?

  • Hold and swipe fast across things to slice them. A finger works too.
  • Cut right through the middle for a PERFECT slice and double points.
  • Slice 3 or more in one swipe for a COMBO. Iron blocks need 2 cuts.
  • Never cut a dragon egg! The baby dragon gets grumpy and steals 5 seconds.

Grab an ⌛ hourglass for slow-motion. Gold coins are worth a lot!
