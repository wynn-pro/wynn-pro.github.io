# Slice Master

The dojo throws logs, bamboo, lanterns, pots and iron blocks into the air, and you slice them in
half with your blade. Earn all 8 belts, from White to Black, or see how high you can score in a
60-second blitz.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no downloads, so it runs on GitHub
Pages as is.

## Play it on your computer

Open `index.html` in a web browser. That's it.

## How to play

- **Hold the mouse button and swipe fast** across things to slice them. On a tablet or phone, swipe
  with your finger. Moving slowly doesn't cut.
- Things split along the exact line you swipe. Cut right through the **middle** so both halves are
  about the same size for a **PERFECT** slice and double points.
- Slice 3 or more things close together for a **COMBO** bonus (10 points for each thing).
- **Never cut a dragon egg!** A grumpy baby dragon pops out and steals 5 seconds.

| Thing | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 🪵 Log, 🎋 Bamboo | 10 |
| 🏮 Lantern, 🏺 Pot | 12 |
| 🎯 Straw target | 15 |
| 🔩 Iron block (needs 2 cuts) | 25 |
| 🪙 Gold coin | 30 |
| ⌛ Hourglass | 5, plus 4 seconds of slow-motion |
| 🥚 Dragon egg | -5 seconds |

## Belt Trials

Each belt gives you 40 seconds to reach a target score. Higher belts throw more things at once,
and more eggs and iron blocks.

| Belt | Score needed |
| --- | --- |
| White | 150 |
| Yellow | 250 |
| Orange | 350 |
| Green | 450 |
| Blue | 560 |
| Purple | 680 |
| Brown | 800 |
| Black | 950 |

If you miss the target you can try that belt again. Your belts are saved on that computer, and
Belt Trials starts at the next belt you haven't earned yet.

## 60-Second Blitz

One minute and no target. It gets busier every 10 seconds. Your best score is saved.
