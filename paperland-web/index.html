PAPERLAND

A whole world cut out of paper. Dig it, build it, and craft the legendary Golden Quill.

  • A / D or arrows to walk, W or Space to jump.
  • Hold the left mouse button on a block to dig it. Click Ink Blots to bop them.
  • Right-click to build with the block in your hand. 1-9 or the mouse wheel picks.
  • E opens your bag. Craft tools there. Some things need a workbench or furnace nearby.
  • At night Ink Blots come out. They hate sunshine and can't appear near torches.

Your world saves by itself on this computer.

🎒 Bag

Click a slot, then another slot, to swap them. The top row is your hotbar.
