# Paperland

A whole world cut out of paper. Dig through grass, stone and caves, build whatever you like, craft
better tools, and keep away from the Ink Blots that come out at night. Your big goal is to craft
the legendary **Golden Quill**.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no downloads, so it runs on GitHub
Pages as is.

## Play it on your computer

Open `index.html` in a web browser. That's it.

## How to play

- **A / D** or the arrow keys to walk, **W**, **Space** or **Up** to jump.
- **Hold the left mouse button** on a block to dig it. Click an Ink Blot to bop it.
- **Right-click** to build with the block in your hand. Right-click with an apple to eat it.
- **1-9** or the mouse wheel picks a hotbar slot.
- **E** opens your bag. Click two slots to swap them, and craft things from the list.
- **M** turns the sound on and off, **Esc** saves and goes back to the title screen.

On a tablet or phone, use the ◀ ▶ ⤒ buttons. The ⛏️ button switches between digging and building.

Your world saves by itself every 15 seconds and when you leave the page.

## The goal list

The bar under the board always tells you what to do next:

1. Collect a log from a tree.
2. Make planks, then a Workbench.
3. Put the Workbench down and craft a Wooden Pickaxe.
4. Dig to stone and craft a Stone Pickaxe.
5. Craft a Furnace from 8 stone.
6. Find iron ore, smelt Iron Bars and craft an Iron Pickaxe.
7. Dig deep for gold ore and smelt 3 Gold Bars.
8. Find a glowing Crystal near the very bottom of the world.
9. Bop Ink Blots and collect 5 Ink.
10. Craft the Golden Quill!

## What you can dig

| Block | Pickaxe needed | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grass, dirt, sand, logs, leaves | none | the surface |
| Stone, coal ore | Wooden | just under the dirt |
| Iron ore | Stone | about 15 blocks down |
| Gold ore | Iron | about 30 blocks down |
| Crystal ore | Iron | right near the bottom |
| Bedrock | can't be dug | the very bottom |

Leaves sometimes drop an **apple**, which heals 3 hearts.

## Ink Blots

Ink Blots hop toward you and take one heart when they bump into you. They come out at night, and
in dark caves at any time. Sunshine dries them up, and they can't appear near a torch, so light up
your house and tunnels! Each one you bop drops 1 or 2 Ink.

If you lose all your hearts you get crumpled up, but you unfold back at the start and keep
everything in your bag. Hearts slowly come back when nothing has hurt you for a while.
