# Tiny Tides

Cast a line from your little boat, fight the fish on the hook and fill your journal with all 12
species, from the tiny Minnow in the shallows to the Old Gulper that lurks in the Abyss at night.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no downloads, so it runs on GitHub
Pages as is.

## Play it on your computer

Open `index.html` in a web browser. That's it.

The game saves itself (on that computer only). Press **Continue** on the title screen to carry on.

## How to play

- **Hold** the mouse (or touch, or the **Space** bar) to charge your cast, and **let go** to throw.
- The lure sinks by itself until your line runs out. **Hold** to reel it back up.
- When a fish bites, a **!** pops up. **Click fast** to hook it!
- Now fight it: **hold** to reel in, and **let go** when the line turns red, or it snaps.
  When the fish pulls, it swims away. If it gets all the way off the right side, it's gone.
  The **FISH** bar shows how much fight it has left.
- Every fish is sold right away for coins. Bigger fish are worth more.
- One day and night lasts 2 minutes. Some fish only come out at **night**.

## The shop

| Gear | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 🎣 Rod | Pull harder before the line snaps |
| 🌀 Reel | Reel in faster |
| 🧵 Line | Sink deeper: 10 m, 20 m, 30 m, then all the way to the Abyss at 40 m |
| 🪱 Bait | Fish notice it from farther away, and rare fish bite more |

## The lake

| Zone | Depth | Fish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shallows | 0–10 m | Minnow, Sunny, Tiger Perch |
| Reef | 10–20 m | Coral Snapper, Puffer, Striped Bass |
| Deep | 20–30 m | Silver Eel, Bluefin, Swordfish |
| Abyss | 30–40 m | Lanternfish, Ghost Fish (night), Old Gulper (night, legendary) |

Catch all 12 to become a **Master Angler**.
