📜 How to play

  • Take turns dropping a piece into a column. It falls to the lowest free spot.
  • Line up your pieces in a row (across, up, or diagonal) to win the round.
  • Win 3 rounds to win the match (best of 5).
  • Each round you get three one-use powers. Using one takes your turn:
    • 💣 Cannonball: destroy one enemy piece. Pieces above fall down.
    • ⚓ Anchor: smash every piece in a column and leave yours at the bottom.
    • 🐙 Kraken: drags the whole bottom row under. Everything drops one row.
  • If a power makes both crews line up at once, the round is a draw.
