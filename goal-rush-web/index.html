❓ How to play

🔴 Red (Player 1)🔵 Blue (Player 2)
MoveWASD←↑↓→
Kick ⚽SpaceEnter
Slide tackle 🦵FRight Shift
Fire shot 🔥E/
Speed boost ⚡Q.
  • 🤖 vs Computer: arrow keys and WASD both move you.
  • 🔥 Fire shot: press it and your boots catch fire for 5 seconds. Your next kick is a flaming rocket! If it hits the other player, they get dizzy.
  • ⚡ Speed boost: run super fast for 3 seconds.
  • 🦵 Slide tackle: slide to steal the ball. Hit the other player and they get dizzy!
  • ⏳ Super moves need time to charge. Watch the circles at the bottom of the screen.
  • 🚩 If the ball goes out, you get a throw-in, corner kick or goal kick.
  • ⏸ Press Esc to pause.
