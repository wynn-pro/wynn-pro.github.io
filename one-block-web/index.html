🟩 One Block Island

You're floating in the sky with just one magic block. Every time you break it, a new one appears!

  • Click / tap the glowing block to mine it.
  • Pick a block in the bar at the bottom, then click a block face to build. Right-click builds too.
  • Pick ⛏ to mine again. Mining your own blocks gives them back.
  • Drag to spin the camera, scroll / pinch to zoom. Keys 1–9 pick slots.
  • Spend 🪙 on a better pickaxe so you mine faster. Find 🎁 chests!
