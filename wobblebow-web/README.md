# Wobblebow

Two floppy ragdoll archers stand on hills across a windy valley and take turns shooting at each
other until somebody falls over. Climb the ladder of 8 rivals, or play a friend on the same computer.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no downloads, so it runs on GitHub
Pages as is.

## Play it on your computer

Open `index.html` in a web browser. That's it.

## How to play

- **Drag back** anywhere on the screen, like pulling a bowstring, then **let go** to shoot.
  The farther you drag, the stronger the shot. The dotted guide shows how the arrow starts off.
- Or use the keyboard: **↑ ↓** to aim, **← →** for power, **Space** to shoot.
- The **wind** changes every turn and pushes arrows sideways. The guide doesn't show the wind!
- Where you hit matters:

| Hit | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Head | 40 (**HEADSHOT!**) |
| Body | 22 |
| Arms and legs | 12 |

Archers wearing a pot (or a helmet) only take body damage from headshots.

## Ladder vs CPU

Beat 8 rivals in a row, from Twig to The Grand Wobbler, to win the Golden Bow. Each one has more
health and better aim, and they get better at judging the shot the longer a duel goes on. Lose once
and the run starts over. Your best run is saved on that computer.

After every win, pick one of 3 perks:

| Perk | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 💪 Thick Skin | +30 max health (up to 3 times) |
| 🪨 Heavy Tips | Arrows hit 25% harder (up to 3 times) |
| 🦅 Eagle Eye | Longer aiming guide (up to 2 times) |
| 🏋️ Big Bow | Arrows fly 12% faster and farther (up to 2 times) |
| 🔱 Triple Shot | Shoot 3 arrows at once, 60% damage each |
| 🍎 Snack Pouch | Heal a third of the damage you deal |
| 🎯 Steady Hands | Wind pushes your arrows half as much |
| 🪖 Iron Pot | Headshots only hurt you like body hits |

## 2 Players

Take turns on the same computer. Player 1 is on the left, Player 2 on the right.
The score keeps counting until you go back to the menu.
