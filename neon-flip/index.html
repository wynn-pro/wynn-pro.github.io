Stack blocks in space. But watch out: gravity can flip to any side of the board!

🪙 0 coins · Best score 0

How to play
  • Fill a whole line to clear it. The glowing edge of the board is the floor.
  • At random times a 3‑2‑1 countdown appears and gravity flips: down, up, left or right. Every block falls to the new floor, and lines that fill up count double!
  • Arrow keys follow the screen: the arrow pointing at the floor makes the block fall faster, the opposite arrow turns it, and the other two move it.
  • Clear lines to call a boss. Clear more lines to hurt it before its junk rows and meteors bury you.
  • Spend coins in the shop on lasers, slow-mo, an extra hold slot and spaceship pets that zap blocks for you.
